# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the main stakeholders who may have an interest in, influence on, or relationship with BuildWise.

The analysis is part of the initial Product Discovery phase and is intended to provide an early understanding of stakeholder needs and influence.

The stakeholder landscape may be refined as further user research and product discovery are conducted.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Categories

The initial stakeholders are grouped into three main categories:

1. Individual Users
2. Educational and Training Stakeholders
3. Internal BuildWise Stakeholders

---

## 3. Individual Users

Individual users are the primary focus of BuildWise.

### 3.1 Software Engineering Students

Software Engineering students are the main initial users of BuildWise, particularly students working on graduation or practical software projects.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Understand what to do throughout the software project lifecycle.
* Connect Software Engineering concepts with practical project work.
* Receive structured guidance and clear next steps.
* Organize project artifacts and documentation.
* Track project progress.
* Gain confidence in their engineering decisions.
* Reduce uncertainty when moving from one project stage to another.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Their experiences, feedback, and project needs can directly influence the product workflow, features, and guidance provided by BuildWise.

**Current Relevance:** Primary

---

### 3.2 Recent Software Engineering Graduates

Recent graduates working on personal, portfolio, or early professional software projects may use BuildWise to apply Software Engineering practices in practical projects.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Apply academic Software Engineering knowledge to real projects.
* Follow a structured engineering workflow.
* Organize requirements, analysis, design, and documentation.
* Improve confidence in engineering decisions.
* Build stronger practical Software Engineering experience.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Their feedback may help identify needs that differ from those of university students and may influence future product development.

**Current Relevance:** Secondary / Future

---

### 3.3 Software Engineering Trainees and Learners

Software Engineering trainees and other learners working on practical projects may use BuildWise to practice building complete software systems using Software Engineering practices.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Apply Software Engineering concepts through practical projects.
* Receive structured project guidance.
* Understand how different engineering activities connect.
* Track project progress and completed work.
* Develop practical experience.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Their experiences may help validate whether BuildWise can support learning and practical project development beyond university graduation projects.

**Current Relevance:** Secondary / Future

---

## 4. Educational and Training Stakeholders

These stakeholders may become more relevant as BuildWise expands beyond individual users.

### 4.1 Universities and Academic Programs

Universities and Software Engineering academic programs may potentially use BuildWise to support practical project development and provide a structured environment for student projects.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Support students in applying Software Engineering practices.
* Improve organization and visibility of student projects.
* Support project supervision and evaluation.
* Monitor student or team progress.
* Encourage consistent engineering practices.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Academic programs could influence requirements for educational use, project supervision, evaluation, and reporting.

**Current Relevance:** Future

---

### 4.2 Instructors, Project Supervisors, and Evaluators

Instructors, graduation project supervisors, and evaluators may interact with BuildWise in educational environments.

Their role may include guiding students, reviewing project artifacts, monitoring progress, providing feedback, or evaluating project work.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Understand student project progress.
* Review relevant project artifacts and documentation.
* Provide structured feedback.
* Support students throughout the project lifecycle.
* Evaluate whether expected project activities or deliverables have been completed.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Their feedback can influence how BuildWise supports project supervision, review, feedback, and evaluation.

**Current Relevance:** Future

---

### 4.3 Training Organizations

Training organizations may potentially use BuildWise to guide groups of learners through practical Software Engineering projects.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Guide groups of learners through structured project workflows.
* Monitor project progress.
* Support mentors and trainers.
* Organize projects and learner activities.
* Provide a consistent practical Software Engineering process.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Training organizations may influence future requirements related to learner management, project management, mentoring, permissions, and reporting.

**Current Relevance:** Future

---

### 4.4 Trainers and Mentors

Trainers and mentors working with Software Engineering learners may use BuildWise to support project guidance and review.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Follow learner progress.
* Review project work and artifacts.
* Provide feedback.
* Identify areas where learners need additional guidance.
* Help learners follow an appropriate Software Engineering process.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

Their practical experience and feedback may influence future mentoring, review, and guidance features.

**Current Relevance:** Future

---

## 5. Internal BuildWise Stakeholders

### 5.1 BuildWise Product and Development Team

The BuildWise Product and Development Team is responsible for defining, building, testing, and evolving the platform.

At the current stage, BuildWise is being developed individually. As the project evolves, this stakeholder group may include additional contributors.

**Interests and Needs:**

* Build a useful and technically sound product.
* Validate the product idea and assumptions.
* Prioritize development work.
* Maintain the quality and sustainability of the system.
* Learn from user feedback and improve the product iteratively.

**Influence on BuildWise:**

This stakeholder has direct influence over product decisions, technical decisions, priorities, and future development.

**Current Relevance:** Internal / High

## 7. Initial Stakeholder Prioritization

Based on the current product discovery hypothesis:

### Primary Focus

**Software Engineering Students**

The initial BuildWise product is primarily focused on individual Software Engineering students building software projects.

### Secondary / Future Focus

The following stakeholders may become relevant as the product expands:

* Recent Software Engineering Graduates
* Software Engineering Trainees and Learners
* Universities and Academic Programs
* Instructors, Project Supervisors, and Evaluators
* Training Organizations
* Trainers and Mentors

### Internal Focus

**BuildWise Product and Development Team**

Responsible for building and evolving the platform.
